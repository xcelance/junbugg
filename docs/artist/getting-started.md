# Artist Guide — Getting Started

> **For:** Artists on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Setting up your profile, becoming an artist, accessing your dashboard

---

## 1. What It Means to Be an Artist

As an artist on JunBugg, you can:

- ✅ Receive **donations** from fans
- ✅ Create **fan club** subscriptions (monthly paid memberships)
- ✅ Host **paid events** (one-time ticket sales)
- ✅ Get **paid automatically** via Stripe Connect
- ✅ View your **dashboard** with earnings & stats

---

## 2. Switch to Artist Profile

### Step 1: Log In

1. Go to the JunBugg website
2. Click **Login** (top-right)
3. Enter your username and password

### Step 2: Change Your Profile Type

1. Go to your **Profile/Account** page
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Find **Profile Type** or **Account Type**
4. Change from `Personal Profile` to **`Artist Profile`**
5. **Save**

### Step 3: Fill In Your Artist Details

Add information about yourself:

- **Creator Name** / **Stage Name**
- **Bio / About Me** — Tell fans about yourself
- **Services Offered** — What do you offer?
- **Website** — Your personal website
- **Social Links** — Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, etc.
- **Profile Picture** — Upload a photo or logo

## 3. Access Your Artist Dashboard

Your dashboard is where you manage everything:

1. Log in to the site
2. Go to your **Profile**
3. Click the **Dashboard** tab
4. You'll see:
