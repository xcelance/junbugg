# Artist Guide — Fan Clubs

> **For:** Artists on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Creating and managing paid fan club subscriptions

---

## 1. What Is a Fan Club?

A fan club is a **monthly subscription** that fans can join to support you. In exchange, you can offer exclusive content, early access, badges, or anything you choose.

**How it works:**

- Fans pay a monthly fee (set by you)
- Subscription auto-renews each month
- Fans can cancel anytime
- You receive payouts automatically

---

## 2. Create a Fan Club

Fan clubs are created by the site administrator. Contact them to:

1. Create a **Membership Pro plan** with:
   - **Recurring:** Yes
   - **Duration:** 1 Month
   - **Price:** Your chosen amount (e.g., $5)
2. Link the plan to your artist account

Available price tiers:

| Tier | Monthly Price | Typical Perks |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| Basic | $3–$5 | Early access, badge |
| Premium | $10–$15 | Exclusive content, shoutouts |
| VIP | $25+ | One-on-one, merch discounts |

## 3. How Fans Join

1. Fan visits your profile or the fan club page
2. Clicks **Join Fan Club**
3. Chooses a payment method (Stripe)
4. Completes payment
5. Membership is activated immediately
6. Fan sees a badge or indicator on their profile

---

## 4. Manage Subscribers

### View Your Fan Club Members

Your dashboard shows:

- **Number of active subscribers**
- **Monthly recurring revenue**
- **List of subscribers** (names/emails)

### Cancel a Subscription

To cancel a fan's subscription:

1. Contact the site administrator
2. Provide the fan's name or email
3. The admin will cancel the subscription
4. The fan will have access until the end of the paid period

---

## 5. Pricing Tips

| Price | What To Expect |
|-------|---------------|
| $3–$5 | More subscribers, lower barrier |
| $10–$15 | Fewer but more committed fans |
| $25+ | Dedicated supporters, higher expectations |

---

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I change my fan club price?**  
A: Yes — contact the administrator. Existing subscribers keep the old price until they cancel.

**Q: What happens when a fan's card declines?**  
A: Stripe retries for a few days. If it fails, the subscription is cancelled.

**Q: Can fans get refunds?**  
A: You can choose to refund through the site administrator.
