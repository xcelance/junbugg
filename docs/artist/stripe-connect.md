# Artist Guide — Connecting Stripe (Getting Paid)

> **For:** Artists on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Setting up Stripe to receive payouts, checking payout status

---

## 1. Why Connect Stripe?

To receive money from donations, fan clubs, and paid events, you need to connect a **Stripe Express** account. Stripe handles all payments and deposits money directly into your bank account.

---

## 2. Connect Your Stripe Account

### Step 1: Go to Your Dashboard

1. Log in to the site
2. Go to your **Profile → Dashboard**
3. Find the **Payout Settings** section

### Step 2: Click "Connect with Stripe"

1. Click the **Connect with Stripe** button
2. A new window or tab will open (allow popups if blocked)

### Step 3: Complete Stripe Express Onboarding

Fill in the Stripe Express form:

| Section | What You Need |
|---------|---------------|
| **Personal Details** | Full legal name, date of birth |
| **Address** | Home/business address |
| **ID Number** | Last 4 digits of SSN (US) or equivalent |
| **Bank Account** | Routing & account numbers for payouts |
| **Business Info** | What type of business (Individual/Company) |

### Step 4: Submit

Once submitted, Stripe will verify your information. This usually takes a few minutes.

---

## 3. Check Your Payout Status

After connecting, your dashboard shows:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ✅ **Active** | You can receive payouts — all good! |
| ⏳ **Pending** | Information submitted, waiting for Stripe verification |
| ⚠️ **Requirements Due** | Stripe needs more information — click to complete |
| ❌ **Disabled** | Something is wrong — check requirements or contact support |

---

## 4. How Payouts Work

### Payout Schedule

| Period | When You Get Paid |
|--------|------------------|
| First payout | 7–14 days after first payment |
| Ongoing payouts | Daily or weekly (depends on Stripe settings) |

### Payout Amount

```
Fan pays $10.00
      │
      ▼
Stripe takes fee (~2.9% + $0.30)
      │
      ▼
Platform takes commission (configurable %)
      │
      ▼
You receive the rest → Your bank account
```

### View Your Payouts

1. Log in to [Stripe Express](https://express.stripe.com/)
2. You'll see:
   - **Balance** — Available to withdraw
   - **Payouts** — History of deposits to your bank
   - **Transactions** — Individual donations/subscriptions

---

## 5. Disconnecting Stripe

To disconnect your Stripe account:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Payout Settings**
2. Click **Disconnect Stripe**
3. Confirm

> ⚠️ You will stop receiving payouts until you reconnect.

---

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Connect with Stripe" not showing | Go to your Dashboard — make sure you're logged in as an artist |
| Onboarding window doesn't open | Allow popups for this website |
| "Country not supported" | Contact the site administrator |
| Payouts not arriving | Check Stripe Express dashboard for requirements |
| "Verification failed" | Contact Stripe support directly |
