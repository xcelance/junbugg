# Artist Guide — Managing Donations

> **For:** Artists on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Enabling donations, viewing received donations, tracking earnings

---

## 1. Enable Donations

Before fans can donate to you, donations must be enabled:

1. Log in to the site
2. Go to your **Profile → Dashboard**
3. In the **Payout Settings** section, find **Donations**
4. Toggle the switch to **ON**

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Payout Settings                           │
│                                            │
│  Stripe Account: ✅ Connected              │
│  Donations:       [🔘 ON ]  [○ OFF]       │
│  Total Received:  $150.00                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. How Fans Donate

When donations are enabled, fans will see a **Donate** section on your profile page:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  💝 Support [Artist Name]            │
│                                      │
│  Choose amount:                      │
│  [ $5 ]  [ $10 ]  [ $25 ]  [ $50 ]  │
│  Custom: [__________]                │
│                                      │
│  [ 💳 Donate ]                       │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Fans can:
- Choose a preset amount ($5, $10, $25, $50)
- Enter a custom amount (minimum $0.50)
- Pay with credit/debit card via Stripe

---

## 3. View Your Donations

### On Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:

- **Total donations received** — Sum of all completed donations
- **Recent donations** — Latest donors and amounts

### In Stripe Express

1. Log in to [Stripe Express](https://express.stripe.com/)
2. Go to **Payments** to see:
   - Each donation
   - Amount after fees
   - Status
   - Date

---

## 4. Donation Notifications

When someone donates, you'll receive an email notification with:

- Donor name (if provided)
- Donation amount
- Message/comment (if included)

---

## 5. Disable Donations

To temporarily stop accepting donations:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Payout Settings**
2. Toggle **Donations** to **OFF**
3. The Donate section will disappear from your profile

Your existing donations are not affected.

---

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I set a minimum donation amount?**  
A: The minimum is $0.50. Contact the administrator if you need a higher minimum.

**Q: Do I get charged fees on donations?**  
A: Stripe charges processing fees (~2.9% + $0.30). The platform may also deduct a commission.

**Q: Can I refund a donation?**  
A: Yes — contact the site administrator to process a refund.

**Q: Are donations anonymous?**  
A: The donor can choose to donate anonymously. If logged in, their name may be shown to you but not publicly.
