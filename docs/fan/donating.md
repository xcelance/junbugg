# Fan Guide — How to Donate

> **For:** Fans / Users on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Donating to artists, payment methods

---

## 1. How to Donate

1. Go to an **Artist's profile page**
2. Look for the **Donate** section:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  💝 Support [Artist Name]            │
│                                      │
│  Choose amount:                      │
│  [ $5 ]  [ $10 ]  [ $25 ]  [ $50 ]  │
│  Custom: [__________]                │
│                                      │
│  [ 💳 Donate ]                       │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

3. Choose an amount:
   - Click a **preset amount** ($5, $10, $25, $50)
   - Or enter a **custom amount** (minimum $0.50)
4. Click **Donate**
5. You'll be taken to **Stripe Checkout** (secure payment page)
6. Enter your card details
7. Click **Pay**

## 2. Stripe Checkout

When you click Donate, you'll be redirected to Stripe Checkout:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  💳 Pay [Artist Name]               │
│                                      │
│  Amount: $10.00                      │
│                                      │
│  Card number  [____________________] │
│  Expiry       [____]  CVC [____]     │
│  Cardholder   [____________________] │
│                                      │
│  [ 💳 Pay $10.00 ]                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Your card details are safe** — Stripe handles all payment processing. JunBugg never sees your card number.

## 3. After Donating

- ✅ You'll be redirected back to the artist's page
- ✅ A success message confirms your donation
- ✅ The artist receives a notification (anonymous if you're not logged in)
- ✅ Your payment receipt is available in your Stripe email

---

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need an account to donate?**  
A: No — you can donate as a guest. Logging in lets the artist know who you are.

**Q: Is my payment info safe?**  
A: Yes. All payments go through Stripe's secure platform. We never store your card details.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**  
A: Contact the site administrator for refund requests.

**Q: Why isn't the Donate button showing?**  
A: The artist may have donations turned off, or they haven't set up their Stripe account yet.

**Q: What currencies are supported?**  
A: Currently USD. More currencies may be added later.

**Q: Can I leave a message with my donation?**  
A: Yes — there's a comment field on the donation form.
