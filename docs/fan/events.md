# Fan Guide — Event Access

> **For:** Fans / Users on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Buying tickets to paid events

---

## 1. What Are Paid Events?

Artists can host paid events that require a **one-time ticket purchase** to access. Examples:

- 🎵 Listening parties for new music
- 🎤 Live performances
- 📹 Exclusive content premiers
- 🎓 Workshops

---

## 2. Buy a Ticket

1. Find the **event** on the artist's profile or events page
2. Click **Buy Ticket** or **Get Access**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  📅 [Event Name]                     │
│                                      │
│  Artist: [Artist Name]               │
│  Date:   June 30, 2026 — 8:00 PM    │
│  Price:  $5.00                       │
│                                      │
│  [ 🎫 Buy Ticket ]                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

3. You'll be taken to Stripe Checkout
4. Enter your payment details
5. Click **Pay**
6. ✅ You're registered for the event!

## 3. After Buying a Ticket

- ✅ You'll receive event access details (link, password, etc.)
- ✅ A confirmation email is sent
- ✅ The event appears in your **My Events** section

---

## 4. My Events

View your purchased events:

1. Log in to the site
2. Go to **Profile → My Events**
3. See all events you've bought tickets for
4. Access event links and details

---

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get a refund if I can't attend?**  
A: Contact the site administrator. Refunds are handled case-by-case.

**Q: What if the event is cancelled?**  
A: You'll receive a full refund automatically.

**Q: Can I buy tickets for someone else?**  
A: Contact the site administrator.

**Q: Do I need an account to buy tickets?**  
A: Yes — you need to be logged in to purchase event access.

**Q: Will I get a reminder before the event?**  
A: This depends on the artist. Check the event page for details.
