# Fan Guide — Joining Fan Clubs & Subscriptions

> **For:** Fans / Users on JunBugg  
> **Covers:** Subscribing to artists, managing your subscriptions

---

## 1. What Is a Fan Club?

A fan club is a **monthly subscription** to support your favorite artist. You pay a small fee each month, and the artist gets your support.

**Benefits may include:**

- Exclusive content
- Special badge on your profile
- Early access to events
- Shoutouts from the artist

---

## 2. How to Join a Fan Club

1. Visit the **Artist's profile page** or **Fan Club page**
2. Find the **Join Fan Club** section:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🎵 Join [Artist Name] Fan Club     │
│                                      │
│  Price: $5/month                     │
│                                      │
│  [ 🎵 Subscribe Now ]                │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

3. Click **Subscribe Now**
4. You'll be taken to Stripe Checkout
5. Enter your payment details
6. Click **Pay**
7. ✅ You're now a fan club member!

## 3. How Billing Works

| Detail | How It Works |
|--------|--------------|
| **First payment** | Charged immediately when you subscribe |
| **Recurring** | Charged automatically each month |
| **Cancellation** | You can cancel anytime — access continues until end of paid period |
| **Failed payment** | Stripe retries for a few days. If it fails, subscription is cancelled |

---

## 4. Manage Your Subscriptions

### View Your Active Subscriptions

1. Log in to the site
2. Go to your **Profile → My Subscriptions**
3. You'll see:
   - Which artists you're subscribed to
   - Monthly price
   - Next billing date

### Cancel a Subscription

1. Go to **Profile → My Subscriptions**
2. Find the subscription you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel**
4. Confirm

You'll still have access until the end of the current billing period.

---

## 5. Upgrade or Change Plans

Some artists may offer multiple fan club tiers:

| Tier | Price | Perks |
|------|-------|-------|
| Basic | $3/month | Badge, early access |
| Premium | $10/month | Everything above + exclusive content |
| VIP | $25/month | Everything above + monthly call |

To upgrade or downgrade, contact the site administrator.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use a different card for subscriptions?**  
A: Yes — update your payment method in Stripe when prompted.

**Q: What happens if my card expires?**  
A: Stripe will notify you to update your payment method. The subscription will retry for a few days.

**Q: Can I gift a subscription to someone?**  
A: Contact the site administrator for gifting options.

**Q: I want to support multiple artists — can I join multiple fan clubs?**  
A: Yes — you can subscribe to as many artists as you want.

**Q: Are subscriptions refundable?**  
A: Contact the site administrator for refund requests.
